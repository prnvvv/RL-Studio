# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as
contributors and maintainers pledge to making participation in our project
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
sexual orientation, religion, or career level.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our
community:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual
  advances or attention
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without permission
- Conducting in good faith elsewhere
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered appropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and in public spaces
when an individual is representing the project or its community. Examples
of representing the project include using an project email address,
posting via a social media account designated as official, or acting as an
authorized representative of an organized group.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may
be reported by opening an issue or contacting the project team at
...@example.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and
will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the
circumstances.

Project maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, edits, releases, and other contributions that are
not aligned with this Code of Conduct, or to temporarily or
permanently ban any contributor for other behaviors deemed inappropriate,
threatening, or damaging.
